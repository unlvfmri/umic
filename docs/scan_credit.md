
# UNLV Scan Credit Policy

The UMIC Scan Credit Policy supports UNLV researchers by providing opportunities for MRI scanning services at no cost or at a reduced rate, subject to proposal review and approval.

---

### Step 1: Eligibility & Scope

!!! note "Eligibility"

    The UMIC Scan Credit Policy is available to all UNLV internal researchers to support functional, structural, and interdisciplinary MRI projects.

---

### Step 2: Proposal Submission

!!! note "Credit Request"

    In their project proposal, researchers will specify the number of scan credit hours requested to execute their study.

    Researchers should follow the proposal submission process outlined on the [New Users](../new-users/) page.

---

### Step 3: Committee Evaluation & Allocation

!!! note "Review & Determination"

    The UMIC fMRI Advisory Committee and the UNLV Division of Research will review the proposal and determine the optimal number of scan credit hours beneficial to successfully support the project, providing scanning services either at no cost or at a reduced rate.

---

### Questions?

For questions about the UNLV Scan Credit Policy, please contact the **UNLV MR Imaging Core (UMIC)** at [fmri@unlv.edu](mailto:fmri@unlv.edu).
